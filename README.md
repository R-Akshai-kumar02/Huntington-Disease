About the Project

Huntington’s disease (HD) is a neurodegenerative disease caused by expansion of CAG repeats in the exon 1 region of the HD gene. These repeats cause the abnormal HTT protein containing expanded polyglutamine repeats that form toxic aggregates inside neurons. Juglans regia L. (walnut) is a medicinal plant containing bioactive compounds. Its active compounds, including polyphenols, flavonoids, and omega-3 fatty acids, can help in the prevention and treatment of neurological disorders. The study aimed to evaluate the potential of phytochemicals from Juglans regia L. against mHTT using an in silico computational approach. Phytochemicals were retrieved from BIMP database and screened for pharmacokinetics and toxicity properties using SwissADME and ProTox-3.0 tool. Pharmacodynamics activities were predicted using the PASS online server. Six compounds passed the screening and a comparative molecular docking against huntingtin protein using AutoDock vina and LibDock protocol in BIOVIA Discovery Studio software. Geranic acid showed the favourable binding interactions near the polyQ region. Molecular dynamics simulation using OpenMM indicates the stability of the Geranic acid-mHTT complex for initial assessment. These findings suggest that Geranic acid may act as a promising lead compound, findings of this research should be determined using multi nanosecond dynamics simulation, in-vitro and in-vivo approach.
